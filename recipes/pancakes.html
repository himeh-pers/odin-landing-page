Fluffy Pancakes

Description

Soft, fluffy pancakes made from a simple pantry-staple batter. Perfect for a lazy weekend breakfast, and easy to double if you're feeding a crowd.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp melted butter
  • Butter and syrup, for serving

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined — a few lumps are fine.
  4. Heat a lightly greased griddle or pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake and cook until bubbles form on the surface, then flip and cook until golden brown.
  6. Serve warm with butter and syrup.
