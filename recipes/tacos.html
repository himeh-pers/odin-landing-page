Beef Tacos

Description

Quick, weeknight-friendly tacos with seasoned ground beef and your favorite toppings. Ready in under thirty minutes and easy to customize for the whole table.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 8 small taco shells or tortillas
  • 1 packet (or homemade) taco seasoning
  • 2/3 cup water
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • Sour cream and salsa, for serving

Steps

  1. Brown the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat, draining excess fat.
  2. Stir in the taco seasoning and water, then simmer for 5 minutes until thickened.
  3. Warm the taco shells or tortillas according to package directions.
  4. Fill each shell with the seasoned beef.
  5. Top with lettuce, cheese, tomato, sour cream, and salsa as desired.
  6. Serve immediately while warm.
