Classic Lasagna

Description

A rich, layered Italian bake of pasta sheets, seasoned meat sauce, and three kinds of cheese. It takes a bit of patience to assemble, but the result is a comforting, crowd-pleasing dinner that reheats beautifully.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 24 oz marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 egg
  • Salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and boil the noodles until al dente.
  2. In a pan, brown the ground beef with the onion and garlic, then stir in the marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. In a bowl, mix the ricotta, egg, half the parmesan, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  4. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking dish, then layer noodles, ricotta mixture, more meat sauce, and mozzarella. Repeat until ingredients are used up, finishing with cheese on top.
  5. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes, then uncover and bake 15 more minutes until bubbly and golden.
  6. Let it rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
